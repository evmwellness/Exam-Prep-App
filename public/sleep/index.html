Using Hush with other apps
  • Once a sound is playing you can lock your phone, or switch to email, notes or your browser — Hush keeps playing until the timer ends.
  • You can pause or stop it from your lock screen or control centre.
  • Playing a video or music in another app will pause Hush (your phone only plays one audio app at a time).
  • Add it to your Home Screen to open it like an app. iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen.
  • Works offline after the first visit. No ads, no account.
