# Affiliate Researcher

Produce a daily report for Veronica. **Two standing priorities come first in
every run, ahead of everything else:**

1. **50%+ commission programs.** Find and rank affiliate programs that pay
   **50% or more**: high-ticket, recurring and lifetime. This is the most
   important section. Spend the most search effort here and go deeper every
   time.
2. **The path to US$100,000/month within 2 months.** Always generate concrete
   income ideas from the day's research, each with the maths, and keep pushing
   toward that target.

After those, cover the organic funnels that are working now. Follow
`affiliate-100k/CLAUDE.md`, especially "Model with evidence", "Work back from
the number" and "Verify before delivering".

## Steps

1. **Read the last report** in `affiliate-100k/research/reports/` (newest file),
   so today's report can say what changed and doesn't repeat the same programs
   without re-checking them.

2. **Research 50%+ programs (the deepest part; at least 6 searches).** Use
   WebSearch, with `extended` for recency.
   - Lifetime-recurring 50%+ (e.g. Systeme.io, Payhip, Stencil, TubeBuddy,
     LeadPages).
   - 12-month 50%+ SaaS (e.g. beehiiv, Kit, GetResponse, Webflow, monday.com).
   - High-ticket 50%+ courses and coaching paying $500–$5,000 per sale (e.g.
     Amazing Selling Machine, Legendary Marketer).
   - ClickBank / Digistore24 / Whop / JVZoo / WarriorPlus offers at 50–75%,
     especially in wellness and make-money-online. Note refund rates and
     compliance risk.
   - **New programs and rate changes** since the last report: launches, raises,
     cuts and closures.
   - **Programs Veronica already owns that have affiliate programs.** If Gmail
     tools are available, search her inbox for purchases and memberships (Whop,
     SamCart/CreatorU, Kajabi, Digistore24, Skool, Stripe receipts). Check
     whether each one offers 50%+ to affiliates. She can promote what she has
     actually used, which is the most credible kind of recommendation. If Gmail
     isn't available, say so and use the list in the last report.
   For each program record: commission %, duration (one-off / 12 months /
   lifetime), approx US$ per referral (state the price you assumed), cookie
   length, network, refund rate if known, and fit for Veronica.

3. **Verify.** For every rate, try the program's own affiliate page (WebFetch).
   Mark each figure **verified (date)** or **unverified (source)**. Never invent
   income figures, conversion rates or testimonials. Treat claims from course
   sellers and tool vendors as claims, not facts.

4. **Research organic funnels (lighter; at least 4 searches).** Cover
   short-form video + comment-to-DM, YouTube search, Pinterest → email,
   newsletter, SEO, TikTok Shop and communities. Score each funnel model 1–5
   on commission economics, time to first sale, compounding, effort and
   evidence quality.

5. **Generate $100k/month-in-2-months ideas.** Give at least 3 ideas, refreshed
   with what you found today. For each, show:
   `sales or referrals needed = 100,000 ÷ commission per sale`, then work
   backwards to traffic, leads and conversion. Say which inputs are
   assumptions. Say plainly whether the idea is realistic within 60 days, and
   what would have to be true for it to work. Always consider:
   - (a) a high-ticket 50% affiliate offer
   - (b) Veronica's own product with a 50% affiliate/JV army
   - (c) a recurring 50–60% stack
   - (d) combinations of these
   Give one concrete action for this week and the metric that shows it worked.
   Be honest: if no route gets to $100k in 60 days, say what monthly figure is
   realistic and how long $100k would take.

6. **Write the report** to `affiliate-100k/research/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md` using
   the template below (Australia/Brisbane date).

7. **Deliver:** commit and push the report on the current branch. Reply with
   the 50%+ table, the $100k ideas, the verdict, and all sources as markdown
   links.

## Rules

- 50%+ programs and the $100k ideas are never skipped or shortened, even on
  quiet news days.
- Organic first. Paid ads only as a later scale-up, with the cost shown.
- Prefer recurring and lifetime commissions and owned audiences (email).
- Don't change the verdict without new evidence. Say what caused any change.
- Compliance: FTC disclosure on every recommendation, no income claims she
  can't prove, opt-in-only email. Flag risky offers (high refunds, unclear
  health claims).
- Ask before any outward-facing action: posting, emailing, joining programs or
  spending money.
