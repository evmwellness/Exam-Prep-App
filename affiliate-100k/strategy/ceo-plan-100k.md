# CEO Plan: Route to A$100k/month
_Prepared 19 Oct 2026. Based on your Claude sessions, this repo, your inbox (courses, memberships, tools) and web research. Market figures come from secondary sources unless marked; see "Sources"._

## 1. The decision

**Build "GP Exam Prep" into a doctor-led medical exam-prep company, sold to registrars and IMGs, with affiliates (registrar ambassadors, med-ed creators and IMG coaches) doing much of the selling.**

This is the most plausible of your ideas because:
- The product is **already built and deployed**: a 250+ question AKT/KFP bank, exam simulator, weak-area analytics, plus check Prep (60 check-unit summaries with AKT and KFP practice). Nothing else you own is this close to sellable.
- **Buyers are proven to pay a lot.** Competitors charge about A$600–A$2,975 (GP Academy A$2,375–2,975, GPEX ~A$830–2,550, PassGP ~A$595).
- **The buyers are easy to find.** About 1,100–1,200 RACGP AKT candidates sit per sitting (unverified for 2026.1), plus ~4,500 AMC MCQ candidates a year (2022–23). They gather in a handful of Facebook groups, training programs and Instagram/TikTok med-ed accounts, which suits affiliates.
- It's **exactly what you asked for**: a product affiliates can sell, with high income potential for you.
- It **fits your life**. It's self-paced software you can run around your family.

## 2. Revenue model: how A$100k/month adds up (a steady-state target, not month 2)

| Revenue line | Price (assumed) | Volume needed per month | A$/month |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. AKT + KFP Pass Pack (12-month access) | A$497 | ~50 sales (≈25% of ~2,300 AKT sitters/yr) | ~24,000 |
| B. AMC MCQ Pass Pack for IMGs (new, mostly reuses the AKT bank) | A$397 | ~56 sales (≈15% of ~4,500/yr, plus overseas) | ~22,000 |
| C. "check Prep" CPD subscription for practising GPs | A$19/mo | 1,500 subscribers | ~28,500 |
| D. Practice / training-provider licences (clinics that employ registrars) | A$2,500/mo | 10 licences | ~25,000 |
| **Total gross** | | | **≈ A$100,000** |

Affiliates take 30–50% of the sales they refer, and payment fees take ~3%. If about 40% of sales come through affiliates, your net is roughly **A$80–85k**. To net A$100k, push volume about 20% higher.

**Seasonality:** A and B spike in the 8–12 weeks before each sitting. C and D smooth out the months in between.

**Realistic timeline (stated honestly):** first revenue in weeks 3–6; A$10k months by month 2–3; A$40–50k by month 6 (once the AMC product and subscription are live); **A$100k/month around months 12–18**. Pre-sales and affiliates make it faster than a typical start, but no credible route gets this market to A$100k in 60 days.

## 3. The plan

### Phase 0: Make it sellable (weeks 1–2, 19 Oct – 1 Nov)
1. **Clinical review sprint.** Check and correct the AKT/KFP questions against current guidelines (you, or a paid FRACGP reviewer at about A$80–120/hr). Add "Reviewed by Dr …, FRACGP" to the product. This is non-negotiable before charging.
2. **IP and compliance check.** Remove anything that implies RACGP affiliation or reproduces gated check content. Keep "independent, not affiliated" disclaimers. Get a one-hour legal check on the use of the "check" name.
3. **Paywall + checkout.**
   - Add accounts + payments to the PWA. Simplest option: Stripe Checkout with an unlock code. Alternatives: SamCart (already paid until Mar 2027, has an Affiliate Center) or Whop (your seller account is ready).
   - **Pick SamCart.** It's already paid for, has a built-in affiliate centre and takes Australian payments.
4. **Landing page + free funnel:**
   - **Free 20-question AKT diagnostic** (the ScoreApp-style quiz from the Daniel Priestley blueprint you downloaded) → instant weak-area report → email sequence.
   - **Email platform: Systeme.io** (60% lifetime affiliate, and it replaces the suspended AWeber).

### Phase 1: First revenue + affiliate engine (weeks 3–8, 2 Nov – 13 Dec)
1. **Founding-member pre-sale:** 100 seats at **A$297** (normally A$497), sold for the next AKT sitting (check RACGP dates). **Target: 30 sales ≈ A$8.9k.**
2. **Affiliate program (the "Ambassador" tier)**, run through SamCart's Affiliate Center:
   - Registrars who passed: 30% commission plus free access.
   - Med-ed creators and IMG coaches: 40% commission.
   - Study-group leaders: a group discount code, plus 30% to the leader.
   - Recruit 20 ambassadors using the outreach scripts from Monetise (Iman Gadzhi).
   - **Target: 10 active ambassadors by 13 Dec.**
3. **Daily organic content:** one AKT question a day as a Reel/TikTok/Short ("comment AKT for the answer + free diagnostic") with ManyChat-style DM automation. This uses your Reels courses, Higgsfield and ElevenLabs.
   - **Target: 1,000 diagnostic sign-ups by 13 Dec.**
4. **Community seeding:** answer questions and share free mini-mocks in the GP registrar and IMG Facebook groups, within each group's rules.

### Phase 2: Expand the product line (months 3–6, Jan – Apr 2027)
1. **AMC MCQ Pass Pack** for IMGs: a global audience, and most of the content can be adapted from the AKT bank. Launch through IMG coaches as affiliates at 40%.
2. **check Prep CPD subscription** (A$19/mo): monthly summaries of new units, quizzes and a CPD log export.
3. **Pass-rate data:** survey users after the results come out. Real pass-rate numbers become your strongest (and honest) marketing.

### Phase 3: Scale to A$100k (months 6–12+)
1. **Practice/training-provider licences** (line D): one pilot practice, then a case study.
2. **Paid ads**, only after organic conversion is proven. Superior Ads is already paid for.
3. **Hire:** a part-time clinical content editor and a VA for affiliate management, paid from revenue.

## 6. Risks and how to handle them
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI-generated clinical content has errors, which damages your reputation | Full clinical review before launch, a "report an error" button, and named reviewer credit |
| RACGP trademark/IP ("check", exam names) | Independent-provider disclaimer; no gated content; legal check in Phase 0 |
| Small, seasonal market (~2,300 AKT sitters/yr) | Add AMC/IMG (global), CPD subscription and practice licences |
| Strong incumbents (GP Academy, GPEX, GP Institute, free iatroX) | Compete on price (A$297–497 vs A$830–2,975), mobile-first design, offline use and short 20-minute blocks for busy parents/registrars |
| Health-advertising rules | Exam prep isn't a health service, but avoid testimonials about clinical outcomes and keep claims about pass rates truthful and evidenced |
