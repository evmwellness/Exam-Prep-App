# Make $100,000 Per Month Online

Owner: **Veronica**, running **Aurvaine**.

## Mission

Study and model affiliate marketers who currently run funnels earning around
$100,000/month. Turn what we learn into repeatable funnels and automations for
Aurvaine, on an aggressive two-month timeline.

## What lives here

- `research/`: notes on top affiliate marketers. Cover their funnels, traffic
  sources, offers, email sequences, hooks and creatives. Put each person or
  funnel in its own file.
- `products/`: high-ticket and recurring-commission affiliate products we are
  considering. Record the commission structure, cookie length, network, refund
  rate and EPC where known.
- `automations/`: scripts and workflows. Examples are content pipelines,
  email sequences, lead capture and reporting.
- `funnels/`: Aurvaine's own funnel plans and copy: landing pages, bridge
  pages, opt-ins and follow-up.

Create these folders as needed. Keep this list in sync with what actually
exists.

## Standing priorities (every time)

- **50%+ commission programs come first.** Whenever affiliate programs or
  research come up, focus hardest on programs paying 50% or more (high-ticket,
  recurring, lifetime). Rank them by realistic US$ per referral.
- **Always be generating ideas for US$100,000/month within 2 months.** Tie
  every piece of research back to that target with the maths. Be honest about
  what is realistic in 60 days.
- **Use what Veronica already owns.** Check her inbox for the programs and
  memberships she has bought, and look for affiliate programs among them.
- The daily report is produced by the `affiliate-researcher` skill
  (`.claude/skills/affiliate-researcher/SKILL.md`). Reports go in
  `research/reports/`.

## How Claude should work in this project

- **Model with evidence.** When studying a marketer, separate what can be
  verified (live funnel pages, ad libraries, public income reports, network
  stats) from what they claim. Record the source and the date. Never invent
  income figures, conversion rates or testimonials.
- **Work back from the number.** For every plan, show the maths: traffic ×
  opt-in rate × conversion × commission = monthly income. Say which inputs are
  assumptions and which are measured. Flag where a two-month target depends on
  optimistic inputs.
- **Favour leverage.** Prioritise high-ticket and recurring-commission offers,
  owned audiences (email lists) and automations that compound. Rank them above
  one-off tactics.
- **Automate what repeats.** If a task happens more than twice (content
  drafts, link tracking, reporting, outreach lists), suggest automating it.
- **Ship small, measurable steps.** Each piece of work should end with a
  concrete next action and the metric that will show whether it worked.
- **Ask before outward-facing actions.** Get confirmation before publishing,
  emailing, posting or spending money.

## Rules: verify before delivering

Run a verification pass on every output before handing it to Veronica.

- **Visual outputs** (pages, funnels, dashboards, graphics, emails): render
  them, take screenshots and include them in the reply as proof the check is
  done. Check desktop and mobile widths where relevant. Fix anything broken
  before sharing.
- **Facts and figures** (income numbers, commission rates, prices, program
  terms, dates, claims about marketers): check every claim against a current
  primary source and note the source and the date checked. Flag anything you
  can't verify or that may be out of date rather than presenting it as fact.
- **Code and automations:** run them, or test them on sample data, and show
  the result.
- In each reply, say briefly what was verified and how.

## Compliance (non-negotiable)

- Every affiliate link and recommendation must carry a clear disclosure (FTC
  in the US, and the equivalent rules elsewhere).
- No fake reviews, fabricated results, invented scarcity or misleading income
  claims in Aurvaine copy.
- Follow each affiliate program's terms: brand bidding, email rules, coupon
  sites and so on.
- Follow email law (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL): opt-in only, with a working
  unsubscribe.
- Use the voices of modelled marketers as structural inspiration only. Don't
  copy their copy or creatives word for word.
