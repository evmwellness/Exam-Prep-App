# Unicorn Bakery

A cake-baking game for little bakers aged 2–5. It's a single `index.html` file with no build step
and no dependencies: open it in any browser on a tablet, phone or computer.

Sprinkle the baby unicorn reads every step out loud, so children who can't read yet can play on
their own. Big Unicorn wears oven mitts and helps with the oven and the hot stove.

## How it plays

1. **Pick an age.** This sets how high the counting goes:
   2–3 years count 1–3, 3–4 years count 1–5, 4–5 years count 1–10.
2. **How many layers?** Number tiles show the numeral plus dots (a ten-frame for 1–10), and the
   cake builds layer by layer as it counts.
3. **Pick a tin.** Circle, triangle, heart, square, rectangle, pentagon, hexagon or cone.
4. **Make the batter.** 1 cup of flour, 1 cup of sugar, 1–3 eggs (tap and count each one),
   1 cup of milk, and 1 tablespoon of cocoa for chocolate (or vanilla). Then stir, counting each tap.
5. **Fill the tins.** One tin per layer, counted out loud.
6. **Bake.** "Ovens are very hot! Ask a big person to help." A big person taps the button and
   Big Unicorn puts the tray in. Everyone counts down while it bakes, then a big person takes it out.
7. **Make icing.** 1 block of butter, melted on the stove with a big person's help, then 1 cup of
   icing sugar, whisked.
8. **Stack and ice.** Add each numbered layer and pick its colour: red, blue, green, yellow,
   orange, purple, brown or pink.
9. **Decorate.** Sprinkles, candles (counted as they go on), balloons, letters and numbers.
   Stars are earned at every step, and the finished cake gets a counted summary and confetti.

## Notes

- Voice uses the browser's built-in speech (it picks an Australian or British English voice if
  there is one). The speaker button at the top turns sound and voice on and off.
- Everything runs in the browser, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
